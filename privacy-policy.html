Privacy Policy

KathaPixel QR Barcode Scanner
Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android app handles information. Contact: kamalprabhanshu9@gmail.com.

Camera and images

With your permission, the app uses your camera to read QR codes and barcodes. You may choose an image from your device to scan. Code decoding takes place on your device. The app does not upload camera frames, selected images, or decoded code values to a KathaPixel server.

Scan history and settings

Successfully decoded values, code formats, scan source, and scan times are saved locally in the app. Your continuous scan, sound, and vibration preferences are also saved locally. You can delete individual scans or clear history in the app. Removing the app may remove its local data. The app does not require an account.

Sharing and external apps

When you choose Share, Open, Pay, or Go to site, Android passes the selected value or generated image to the app you choose. That destination may process it under its own privacy policy and may use the internet. You control these actions. CSV history and generated PNG images are saved only when you select a destination.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

The app includes Google's bundled ML Kit barcode scanning component. Although code decoding works offline, ML Kit collects technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics. This may include device manufacturer, model, operating system and available hardware accelerators; app package name and version; a per-installation identifier; processing performance and latency; scanning configuration and input/output sizes; feature events such as initialization and detection; and error codes. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit with HTTPS and is not transferred by ML Kit to third parties. The app does not use this information for advertising. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Network and third parties

Core scanning and code creation work offline, but ML Kit may transmit the technical information described above. The app does not include an advertising SDK. The app does not operate a KathaPixel analytics service. Android, Google Play, and apps you choose to open may handle information according to their own policies.

Contact and changes

For questions or requests about this app, email kamalprabhanshu9@gmail.com. Any change to this policy will be posted with a new update date.
