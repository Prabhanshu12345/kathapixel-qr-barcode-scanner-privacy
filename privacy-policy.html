Privacy Policy

KathaPixel QR Barcode Scanner
Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android app handles information. Contact: kamalprabhanshu9@gmail.com.

Camera and images

With your permission, the app uses your camera to read QR codes and barcodes. You may choose an image from your device to scan. Decoding takes place on your device. The app does not upload camera frames or selected images to a KathaPixel server.

Scan history and settings

Successfully decoded values, code formats, scan source, and scan times are saved locally in the app. Your continuous scan, sound, and vibration preferences are also saved locally. You can delete individual scans or clear history. Removing the app may remove its local data. The app does not require an account.

Sharing and external apps

When you choose Share, Open, Pay, or Go to site, Android passes the selected value or generated image to the app you choose. That destination may process it under its own privacy policy and may use the internet. You control these actions. CSV history and generated PNG images are saved only when you select a destination.

Network and third parties

Core scanning and code creation work offline. The Android build includes Google ML Kit barcode scanning and Android CameraX components. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this build. Android, Google Play, and apps you open may handle technical or account information according to their own policies.

Contact and changes

For questions or requests about this app, email kamalprabhanshu9@gmail.com. Any change to this policy will be posted with a new update date.
